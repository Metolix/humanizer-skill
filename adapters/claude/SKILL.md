# Humanizer

Use `skill/core.md` as the normative behavior.

When invoked: inspect the whole passage; identify audience, purpose, tone, and voice; diagnose clusters rather than individual words; rewrite conservatively; preserve facts, citations, quotations, names, and numbers; then apply `skill/checklist.md`.

Never invent experiences, citations, evidence, opinions, or intentional mistakes. Never promise detector evasion.
