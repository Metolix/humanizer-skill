# Humanizer

Use `skill/core.md` as the normative behavior.

Inspect the complete passage first. Identify audience, purpose, voice, and constraints. Diagnose repeated stylistic patterns rather than individual AI words. Rewrite conservatively. Preserve factual content, citations, quotations, names, and numbers. Run `skill/checklist.md` before returning the result.

If an author sample is provided, treat it as the primary voice reference. Do not manufacture personal experience or intentional mistakes.
