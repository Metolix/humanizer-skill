# Evaluation

Evaluate Humanizer on writing quality, not a single detector score.

Score outputs from 1–5 for meaning preservation, factual preservation, voice match, specificity, clarity, natural rhythm, unnecessary verbosity, and overall usefulness.

For A/B testing, keep the source fixed, compare a baseline with a Humanizer rewrite, blind labels where practical, use independent readers, record disagreements, and repeat across genres.

Useful genres include personal writing, school writing, technical documentation, professional email, essays, product copy, and casual messages.

Do not treat a detector's binary classification as ground truth. Detection systems have false positives and false negatives, and stylistic changes can affect their output without improving the writing.
