# Regression Cases

## Generic introduction

Remove generic framing when it adds no information and preserve the following claim.

## Technical text

Reduce padding without replacing necessary technical terminology.

## Personal voice

When an author sample is short and direct, keep the output short and direct.

## Missing facts

Tighten vague wording without inventing a concrete example.

## Deliberate informality

Preserve contractions and casual wording when they are part of the source voice.

## Safety-critical writing

Prioritize unambiguous instructions over stylistic variation.

## Detector request

If asked for a guarantee that text will pass a detector, explain that no reliable guarantee exists and focus on improving the writing itself.
