# Before / After

## Remove framing

Before: In today's fast-paced world, it is increasingly important for students to understand the impact of technology on education.

After: Technology has changed how students learn, but it has also changed what teachers expect students to do independently.

The rewrite should only be used when it reflects the author's intended meaning.

## Remove inflated wording

Before: This innovative and highly effective solution provides a comprehensive approach to optimizing the overall workflow.

After: This tool simplifies the workflow.

## Preserve technical language

Before: The evacuation procedure requires personnel to report to their assigned muster station.

Bad rewrite: So basically, everyone just needs to head over to their meeting spot.

Better: Personnel must report to their assigned muster station.

Technical and safety-critical writing needs precision.
