# Pattern Catalogue

This is a diagnostic catalogue, not a blacklist.

## Common clusters

- Generic openings and conclusions
- Empty intensifiers
- Abstract nouns where a concrete action is available
- Formulaic transitions
- Repeated symmetrical rhetoric
- Repeated three-part structures
- Summary padding
- Uniform sentence and paragraph rhythm
- Corporate or promotional phrasing in contexts where it does not fit

## Weak signals

These do not prove AI authorship by themselves:

- em dashes
- semicolons
- Oxford commas
- polished grammar
- headings
- bullet lists
- any individual phrase
- unusual vocabulary by itself

Humans use all of these.

## Anti-pattern

Do not run mechanical synonym replacement. Forced slang, fake typos, random fragments, and excessive contractions can make writing worse.
